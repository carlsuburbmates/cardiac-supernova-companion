Author Note • A Sanctuary for Readers

Thank you for walking with me.

In independent publishing, an honest reader review is not a marketing metric. It can be a lighthouse for someone facing a hard season, wondering where to turn or how to keep going.

Dear Reader,

If the reflections and practices in Cardiac Supernova offered you relief, a quiet breath, or a practical compass for carrying life's weight, you can help someone else searching for direction find this work.

Your words do not need to be long or formal. Two honest sentences sharing where this book met you in your life can light the path for someone else.

"People do not connect with your perfection. People connect with your humanity."

Direct review links for Amazon, Goodreads, and Booktopia will activate here upon commercial publication. Until then, thank you for your courage, your tenderness, and for daring to keep your heart soft in a hard world.
